Help with ProcuraSheet v1.1.2

Quick answers for supplier-file conversion, paid plans and billing.

Which files can ProcuraSheet read?

CSV, TSV, TXT and common XLSX workbooks up to 25 MB. Unsafe or unusually large XLSX archive entries are rejected before processing.

Why is a row marked “Blocked”?

Every exported line needs a Shopify SKU or barcode and a positive whole-number quantity. ProcuraSheet also blocks duplicate identifiers, negative costs, invalid tax values and catalog conflicts.

What are the Free, Pro and Business limits?

Free includes 5 exports per calendar month and 2 saved supplier templates. Pro is shown as about $4.50/month and is billed by Paystack as ₦6,000/month. Business is shown as about $9.80/month and is billed as ₦13,000/month; it adds local catalog matching plus reusable Supplier SKU-to-Shopify SKU memory. USD equivalents are approximate and can vary with exchange rates.

Does ProcuraSheet upload my supplier spreadsheet when I pay?

No. Paid-plan verification sends only a ProcuraSheet license token. Supplier files, catalogs and purchase-order rows remain local.

How do I cancel or update billing?

Open ProcuraSheet, choose Manage billing, and use the Paystack-hosted subscription-management page. Removing a license from one browser does not cancel the Paystack subscription.

Does ProcuraSheet need my Shopify password?

No. The extension creates a CSV for you to import manually through Shopify Purchase Orders.

Where are saved mappings stored?

In browser extension storage. You can download a local settings backup and restore it later; the backup excludes your paid license.

Found a bug, billing error or need help?

Use the issue form. Include your browser version, file type, plan and exact error message. Never post confidential supplier files, payment-card details or your ProcuraSheet license token.
