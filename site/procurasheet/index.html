BUILT FOR REPEAT PURCHASING

Useful on the first order. Faster on the next one.

ProcuraSheet remembers supplier layouts locally, keeps unsafe rows visible, and gives growing teams paid options without sending supplier spreadsheets to a cloud converter.

Smart column mapping

Detects SKU, barcode, supplier SKU, quantity, cost and tax—even when the real header starts below supplier notes.

Strict review before export

Blocks missing identities, invalid quantities, duplicates and catalog conflicts instead of silently dropping rows.

Business matching

Business users can load a Shopify product export locally to backfill SKU values by barcode and reuse supplier-SKU corrections.

Private by default

Supplier sheets, product exports and purchase-order rows stay in the browser. Paid verification uses only a ProcuraSheet license token.
