SUBSCRIPTION TERMS

Simple recurring plans.

Last updated: October 6, 2026.

Plans

Free is ₦0. Pro is ₦4,000 monthly or ₦40,000 annually. Business is ₦8,500 monthly or ₦85,000 annually. Paid plans are recurring subscriptions processed by Paystack until cancelled.

Billing

Paystack handles checkout, supported payment methods and recurring charges. Subscription timing follows the selected monthly or annual plan. Taxes, bank charges or currency-conversion charges imposed by a payment provider or issuer may be separate from the displayed subscription price.

Cancellation

Paid subscribers can use the Paystack subscription-management link available through Contractor Clipper to manage or cancel a subscription. A cancelled or otherwise inactive subscription returns to Free access when entitlement status is refreshed or the cached paid entitlement expires.

Your data

Paid status does not move your quote workspace to the billing service. Product, project and client-estimate data remain subject to the Privacy Policy.

Estimates and client responses

Contractor Clipper is a productivity tool. Users remain responsible for reviewing quantities, prices, taxes, scope, terms and legal requirements before relying on or sending an estimate. The Business client-response package records an offline response and quote fingerprint; it is not represented as a qualified electronic-signature or legal-advice service.

Refunds and support

For a billing problem or refund request, contact support with the billing email and transaction reference but never send card details. Requests are handled according to applicable law and the circumstances of the transaction.
