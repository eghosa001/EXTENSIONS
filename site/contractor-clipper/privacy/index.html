PRIVACY POLICY

Local-first by design.

Last updated: October 6, 2026. Contractor Clipper's single purpose is to turn product information that you explicitly scan on supplier websites into project estimates.

Data the extension handles

Contractor Clipper may handle product and website content that you explicitly scan, including product title, SKU or MPN, price, currency, image URL, source page URL and supplier domain. It can also store project/client information you choose to enter, such as client or company name, client email, job address, quote notes, quantities, markup, delivery, labour, discounts, tax, estimate numbers, supplier names and optional business branding.

How website access works

Contractor Clipper does not passively collect your browsing history. It receives temporary access to the current tab when you invoke the extension and reads product information only after you click Scan current product.

Where data is stored

The current release stores workspace data in Chrome local extension storage on your device. Local JSON backup and restore are available from the quote workspace.

Data transmission and sharing

The current release does not transmit clipped website content, page URLs, project/client details, suppliers, quote data or branding to a Contractor Clipper server, analytics provider, advertising provider or other third party. Contractor Clipper contains no advertising or behavioral tracking.

How the data is used

Handled data is used only to provide the disclosed supplier-product-to-estimate workflow: product detection, project storage, supplier memory, quote calculations, backup/restore and user-requested exports.

Permissions

activeTab gives temporary access to the tab you explicitly invoke the extension on. scripting runs the packaged product extractor after you click Scan. storage saves projects, suppliers, quote settings and branding locally. sidePanel displays the clip-to-quote workflow beside the supplier website. The extension does not request broad host permissions.

Backups and deletion

You can export a local JSON backup, remove line items and delete projects from the workspace. Uninstalling the extension removes its extension storage according to Chrome's normal extension-data behavior.

Limited use

Contractor Clipper does not sell handled data, use it for personalized advertising or use it for unrelated purposes. The current release has no Contractor Clipper backend through which a person could read your quote workspace.

Support

For privacy or support questions, use the Contractor Clipper support page.
