# 11 — SheetInvoice

## Product promise
Turn rows in Google Sheets into professional PDF invoices or quotations and optionally email them, without forcing a small business into a full accounting platform.

## Platform
Google Workspace Marketplace — Google Sheets add-on.

## Customer
Freelancers, agencies, consultants, tutors, tradespeople and small businesses already managing work in spreadsheets.

## MVP
- Map spreadsheet columns to invoice fields.
- Generate one or many invoices from selected rows.
- Sequential invoice numbers.
- Business/client details.
- Tax, discount and totals.
- Generate PDF into Google Drive.
- Optional Gmail send.
- Write generated/sent status back to the sheet.
- Reusable Google Docs invoice template.
- No separate CRM or accounting ledger in V1.

## Differentiation
Stay focused on "rows to professional invoice/quote" rather than trying to replace accounting software.

## Demand evidence
Google Workspace Marketplace currently shows:
- Invoice Generator | Bulk Invoices | PDF Receipts — 26K+ users.
- Simple Invoice — 16K+ users.

## Monetization
- Free: limited documents per month.
- Pro target: $5–$10/month.
- Pro: unlimited batches, custom branding/templates, quotes + invoices, scheduled generation and advanced email templates.
- Team/agency tier only after usage proves demand.

## Validation
Measure:
1. Number of PDFs generated per user.
2. Single vs batch usage.
3. Email-send adoption.
4. Repeat monthly use.
5. Willingness to pay for branding/templates rather than accounting features.

## Do not overbuild
No bookkeeping, bank reconciliation, payroll, inventory, payment gateway or tax filing in V1.
