# 02 — BidMatrix Africa

## Product promise
Turn a tender/RFP page or PDF into a compliance matrix that tells a business exactly what evidence, deadlines and eligibility items need attention.

## Customer
African SMEs, contractors, professional-services firms, bid consultants and tender teams.

## MVP
Capture page/PDF text; extract buyer, deadline, tender number, submission method, mandatory documents, experience/turnover requirements, bid security and site-visit rules; create a requirement-by-requirement checklist; let the user mark/upload evidence references; export CSV/PDF checklist.

## Differentiation
Not a tender search engine. It starts after the user finds a tender and prevents missed requirements. Use “potential fit” rather than legal/definitive eligibility claims.

## Architecture
Chrome/Edge extension plus local workspace. Rule-based extraction first, optional LLM extraction later behind explicit user action. Store a reusable company evidence profile locally before adding cloud sync.

## Monetization
Free: 3 tender analyses/month. Pro: $19–29/month. Company: $49/month with shared evidence profile later.

## Launch
Target Nigerian procurement consultants, construction/service SMEs and LinkedIn tender communities. Demonstrate a real tender transformed into a matrix. Success gate: 10 completed tender reviews and evidence that users revisit saved matrices.

## Roadmap
Company document vault, tender-fit scoring, reusable evidence expiry dates, collaboration, bid/no-bid history, then regional procurement-source integrations.
