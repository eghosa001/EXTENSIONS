BUILT FOR REPEAT PURCHASING

Useful on the first order. Faster on the next one.

ProcuraSheet remembers each supplier's column layout and SKU corrections locally in your browser, so recurring imports take less cleanup.

Smart column mapping

Detects SKU, barcode, supplier SKU, quantity, cost and tax—even when the real header starts below supplier notes.

Strict review before export

Blocks missing identities, invalid quantities, duplicates and catalog conflicts instead of silently dropping rows.

Optional catalog matching

Load a Shopify product export to backfill SKU values from matching barcodes and catch mismatches.

Private by default

Your supplier sheets, product exports and mappings stay in the browser. ProcuraSheet does not require Shopify credentials.
