# QuickQuote Pro service and billing terms

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

QuickQuote Pro generates PDF quotations and invoices from information you provide. You are responsible for the accuracy and legal/tax compliance of the amounts and descriptions. This is not professional accounting, financial or legal advice.

**Free:** three generated PDF documents per UTC calendar month. **Pro:** a one-time payment of 300 Telegram Stars gives 30 days of Pro access with a fair-use limit of 500 PDFs per UTC calendar month. Purchasing again extends an existing unexpired term by 30 days. There is no automatic renewal in this version. Paid access only starts after Telegram confirms successful payment.

If you have a billing issue or a purchase is not fulfilled correctly, send /paysupport and describe what went wrong. Eligible refunds may be issued using Telegram's Stars refund API. A refunded purchase's contribution to Pro access is removed. Your Telegram transaction receipt is useful for support.

Deleting your account data cancels remaining access but does not automatically refund a payment. Save your PDF documents to your own storage: the bot is not an invoice archive or backup service.

Service availability depends on Telegram, Cloudflare and their usage limits; uninterrupted operation is not guaranteed. We may update the service and these terms with notice when appropriate.

**Support:** @QuickQuoteProBot /paysupport. **Privacy:** [Privacy notice](PRIVACY.md).
