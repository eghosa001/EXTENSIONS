# 12 — Quote2WhatsApp

## Product promise
Let WooCommerce customers request a quote, let the merchant respond quickly, and move the negotiation to WhatsApp without turning the store into a complex B2B suite.

## Platform
WordPress / WooCommerce plugin.

## Customer
Small wholesalers, custom-product stores, trades, fabricators, furniture sellers, printers, building-material sellers and merchants that negotiate prices before checkout.

## MVP
- Replace/add "Request a Quote" on selected WooCommerce products.
- Quote basket with quantity and notes.
- Customer contact form.
- Merchant receives a structured quote request.
- Generate a WhatsApp message/link containing the quote summary.
- Merchant can set quoted price and expiry.
- Customer can return to WooCommerce checkout with the accepted quote.
- Basic PDF/printable quote after the core flow is stable.

## Differentiation
The market already proves quote-request demand. The wedge is the merchant's real workflow: WooCommerce quote request → fast WhatsApp negotiation → accepted price → checkout.

## Demand evidence
WordPress.org currently shows:
- YITH Request a Quote for WooCommerce — 10,000+ active installs.
- Request a Quote for WooCommerce / Get a Quote Button — 6,000+.
- ELEX WooCommerce Request a Quote — 2,000+.

Reference:
https://wordpress.org/plugins/tags/request-a-quote-button/

## Monetization
- Free: quote button, quote basket and basic request.
- Pro target: $49–$89/year/site.
- Pro: WhatsApp templates, quote revisions, PDF branding, expiry, deposits/accepted-quote checkout and multiple staff numbers.

## Validation
Measure:
1. Quote requests per store.
2. Percentage sent/opened through WhatsApp.
3. Quote-to-checkout conversion.
4. Merchant time to respond.
5. Which Pro feature merchants request first.

## Do not overbuild
No full CRM, WhatsApp inbox replacement, ERP, inventory system or general helpdesk in V1.
