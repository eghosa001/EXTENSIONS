# Chrome Web Store listing — Contractor Clipper v1.0.0

## Name
Contractor Clipper

## Short description
Clip supplier products into branded contractor estimates with markup, labour, tax and exports.

## Detailed description
Contractor Clipper helps contractors, builders, interior designers, renovators, installers and estimators turn supplier product pages into client-ready estimates without copying product details by hand.

Open a supplier product page and click Contractor Clipper. The extension opens in Chrome's side panel, where you can scan the active page, review the detected product name, SKU, price, currency, supplier, source link and image options, then add the product to a project.

Build an estimate with quantities, markup, room/category organization, labour, delivery, discount and tax. Add your company details and logo, then export the project to CSV or Excel, or use Print / PDF for a branded client estimate.

### Highlights
- Persistent Chrome side-panel workflow
- Product name, SKU, price, currency, supplier and source capture
- Multiple detected product images to choose from
- Multiple projects and clients
- Room/area and category organization
- Quantity and markup controls
- Labour, delivery, discount and tax calculations
- Automatic totals
- Saved suppliers
- Company logo and contact branding
- CSV export
- Excel-compatible export
- Printable / Save-as-PDF estimate
- No account required
- Local-first storage
- No blanket website permission

### Privacy
Version 1.0.0 has no backend, ads, analytics, cloud sync or payment collection. Product information is read only after the user invokes the extension on the active tab. Project and estimate data are stored locally in browser extension storage.
