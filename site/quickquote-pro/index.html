Common questions

Does the bot need Telegram Premium?

No. You can use it with an ordinary Telegram account.

Does it use AI tokens?

No AI API is required for document creation. The bot calculates totals and creates PDFs using conventional software.

How do I get help with a payment?

Send /paysupport directly to the bot. A support request will be tracked and handled by the operator.

Can it replace accounting software?

No. The bot is a document generator, not a full accounting, tax or bookkeeping platform. You are responsible for checking document information and keeping your own copies.
