Recurring billing

Paid plans are recurring monthly subscriptions. By subscribing, you authorize the recurring charge shown at Paystack Checkout until you cancel.

Cancellation

You can manage or cancel a paid subscription through the Paystack-hosted subscription-management page opened from ProcuraSheet. Removing the license from a browser does not cancel the subscription.

Plan access

Paid features require a currently active subscription. If a subscription expires, is canceled, cannot be verified, or uses an unrecognized product price, ProcuraSheet can fall back to Free-plan access.

Billing questions and charge errors

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact support promptly. Subscription charges are generally non-refundable once a billing period begins, except for duplicate or erroneous charges, service failures we confirm, or where a refund is required by applicable law. Contact support promptly if you believe a charge is incorrect.

Paid-plan verification does not upload supplier spreadsheets or purchase-order rows. See the Privacy Policy for details.
