ProcuraSheet Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Summary

ProcuraSheet is local-first. Version 1.0 processes supplier files and optional Shopify catalog exports in your browser. It does not operate a server that receives those files.

Data processed by the extension

ProcuraSheet may process supplier spreadsheets, product identifiers, quantities, costs, tax values, supplier names, and optional Shopify product exports that you choose to open. This information is used only to perform the conversion, validation and mapping features you request.

Local storage

Saved supplier templates, header mappings and Supplier SKU-to-Shopify SKU corrections are stored in browser extension storage on your device. This lets repeated imports reuse your previous mapping choices.

Data transmission

Version 1.0 does not upload supplier sheets, product catalogs, purchase-order lines or saved mappings to ProcuraSheet servers. It does not sell personal information and does not use this business data for advertising.

Permissions

The extension requests browser storage permission so it can remember supplier templates and SKU mappings. It does not request browsing-history access, email access, payment information, or Shopify account credentials.

External sites

ProcuraSheet can open Shopify Admin at your request and this website can link to GitHub for support reporting. Those services have their own privacy policies and are not controlled by ProcuraSheet.

Future changes

If future versions introduce accounts, analytics, cloud sync, AI services, payments or Shopify OAuth, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Trademark notice

ProcuraSheet is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Shopify Inc. Shopify is a trademark of Shopify Inc. References to Shopify describe compatibility only.

Support

For questions about this policy or the extension, use the ProcuraSheet support page.
