# MemberFlow — planned second Telegram bot

## Customer
Creators, tutors, private communities and newsletters operating paid Telegram groups/channels.

## Narrow paid MVP (not implemented yet)
- Connect private groups/channels where owner grants administrator permissions
- Invite-link issuance and tracked membership expiry
- Renewal reminders and automated access removal
- Stars-paid B2B administration subscription
- One dashboard for members, failed renewals and expiry dates
- No AI dependency

## Competition and differentiation
InviteMember and similar membership automation services demonstrate willingness to pay for membership administration. Differentiate by clear setup, simple pricing, privacy and reliable access lifecycle—not by cloning every enterprise feature.

## Critical boundary
Do not advertise member-paid recurring memberships until the Stars payment, multi-party payout ownership, Telegram group privileges and renewal/refund rules are correctly designed. Admins must explicitly authorize the bot to control membership. No working code or billing exists here.

## Validation
Interview at least 10 community admins, validate willingness to pay for a particular pain point, then build a minimal paid pilot. Continue only if real adoption is demonstrated.
