NEXT STEP

Import the exported CSV into Shopify

  1. Create or open a Purchase Order in Shopify Admin.
  2. Choose Import, then add the ProcuraSheet CSV.
  3. Review Shopify's matched products, quantities, costs and tax.
  4. Save the purchase order as a draft until you are ready to submit or receive it.
